<p>The Population in Brief (PIB) 2026 publication provides key updates on
Singapore’s population. Here is a snapshot of the key trends:</p>
<p><strong>1. Overall population</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Overall, Singapore’s total population stood at 6.21 million as at June
2026, a 1.6% increase from June 2025. The increase was mainly due to growth
in the non-resident population, particularly an increase in Work Permit
Holders in the Construction sector to support key projects such as Changi
Terminal 5, Marina Bay Sands expansion, and the ramp up of housing supply
and other transport infrastructure.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The citizen population increased by 0.7% from 3.66 million in June 2025
to 3.68 million in June 2026, while the Permanent Resident population remained
relatively stable at 0.55 million in June 2026.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The Non-Resident population stood at 1.98 million in June 2026, an increase
of 3.7% from June 2025.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>2. Citizen marriages</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>There were 21,671 citizen marriages in 2025, 5.6% lower than the 22,955
citizen marriages in 2024. The average number of citizen marriages per
year from 2023 to 2025 (23,000) was lower than from 2016 to 2019 (23,500).<sup>1</sup>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>3. Citizen births</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>There were 26,071 citizen births in 2025, 10.8% lower than the 29,237
citizen births in 2024. The average number of births per year over 2021
to 2025 (29,300) was lower than over 2016 to 2020 (32,500).</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>4. Resident total fertility rate (TFR)</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The resident TFR was 0.87 in 2025, a decrease from 0.97 in 2024.</p>
</li>
</ul>
